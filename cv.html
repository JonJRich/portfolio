Jon Rich

Summary

  • Led the design of Sparx Assessments, taking an experimental feature inside Sparx Maths and turning it into a standalone product. For the first Sparx National Mock, 53,965 students across 406 schools had their marks entered, teachers reported 88% satisfaction, and 63.8% of heads of department said they’d be very disappointed to lose it.
  • Led the discovery and design of a reimagined Insights page for Sparx Science, turning the results of personalised homework into class-level, topic insights with clear confidence levels. Later rolled out to Sparx Maths.
  • Senior product designer at balena, a B2B SaaS platform, leading design across balenaCloud, balenaHub and the marketing site, and building a token-based design system while ARR grew more than 25% year on year.

Experience

Senior product designer Sparx Learning

Jul 2024 to present

Sparx Learning creates digital learning platforms used by millions of students across the UK and internationally. I work across multiple products within agile scrum teams, creating intuitive, accessible learning experiences for students and time-saving tools for teachers.

Sparx Assessments

  • Owned the design and shaped key product decisions, including positioning Sparx Assessments as a standalone product within the Sparx Learning portfolio.
  • Took the product from discovery and decision mapping to launch in 6 months: 2 months of discovery and stakeholder planning, then 4 months to design and build.
  • Walked senior leadership, the CEO and education leads through the trade-offs of 3 product directions, using information architecture and process maps.
  • Designed the complete experience as wireframes first, so structural decisions were settled before high fidelity, then built out a first-phase design system with the brand designer.
  • Built an automated user testing pipeline in Make, running sessions every week and peaking at 25 sessions in a single week, with findings going straight into the backlog.
  • Matched teachers’ mental models, for example ordering the set-up flow differently for revision (class first) and assessments (assessment first).
  • Built a queryable research library in NotebookLM from every testing session, used by engineers and senior stakeholders alike.
  • 92% of schools entered marks for Mock 1, and 98% had their report viewed by a senior teacher within 7 days.

Sparx Science Insights

  • Led a 4-week redesign of the Insights page, aggregating personalised homework up to topic level, with first-time correct rate chosen alongside the education lead.
  • Designed 3 confidence states (not enough data, emerging, confident) so teachers get data early without acting on misleading numbers.
  • Homeworks with significant engagement rose from 5.4% to 7.2%, and praise rose from 13% of feedback posts to 44%.
  • Hand-in visitors who went on to Insights rose from 41% to 44% in Sparx Science, and from 27% to 39% in Sparx Maths, where the page was rolled out.

Across products

  • Maintained and evolved the design systems across products, extending them for Sparx Assessments.
  • Took ownership of projects across products to support team capacity, including Subscriptions, Parent Emails and Sparx Hub, a single point of entry for all of the systems.
  • Pioneered AI-integrated workflows, using Claude Code, Cursor and NotebookLM to prototype from production code and speed up research synthesis.
