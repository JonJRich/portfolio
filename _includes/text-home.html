About

From publishing to product

I’m currently a senior product designer at Sparx Learning, leading design across multiple products in their education portfolio.

Every day is different, but it almost always involves working closely with product to get under the skin of the problems we face, working shoulder to shoulder with engineers to build delightful experiences, and always staying close to the thousands of teachers and 2.5 million students who are our users.

I cut my teeth in publishing, working on the national magazine BBC Science Focus, before – via a side quest into the world of indie publishing – moving over to the world of design agencies. Over a decade I led teams to deliver work for tech clients including Ericsson, Klarna and Salesforce.

One thing was consistent: I was always the designer that ‘did digital’. Five years ago I made the move into product design, joining the team at balena at a time of rapid growth and expanding product offerings.
